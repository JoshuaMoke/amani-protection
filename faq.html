What services does Amani Protection offer?
+

Amani Protection offers four core services: Residential Security, Commercial Security, Event Security, and Bodyguard Services. Each service is tailored to the specific needs of the client and environment. Visit our Services page for full details on each offering.

Do you serve all of Canada?
+

Yes. Amani Protection provides security services to clients across all provinces and territories in Canada. Whether you are in a major city or a more remote area, we can discuss how to best serve your security needs.

Are you available 24/7?
+

Yes. Amani Protection is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. Security needs do not follow a schedule, and neither do we.

How do I request a quote?
+

You can request a quote by filling out our Quote Request Form. Provide as much detail as possible about your service needs, location, dates, and any specific requirements. We aim to respond within 2 hours.

How quickly can I hear back after submitting a request?
+

We aim to respond to all quote requests and inquiries within 2 hours. For urgent security needs, we recommend calling or emailing us directly at info@amaniprotection.com for the fastest response.

Do you provide event security?
+

Yes. Event security is one of our core services. We provide professional coverage for concerts, corporate events, galas, private parties, weddings, and large public gatherings. Our team handles crowd management, access control, VIP protection, and incident response.

Do you provide residential and commercial security?
+

Yes. We provide both residential and commercial security services. Residential security covers homes, condos, and private communities. Commercial security covers businesses, offices, retail locations, and construction sites. Both services can be customized to your specific situation.

What is the difference between event security and bodyguard services?
+

Event security focuses on protecting a venue, location, or gathering by managing access, crowds, and general safety at the site. Bodyguard services (also called close protection) are focused on protecting a specific individual, following them and maintaining their personal safety in any environment.

Are your security personnel trained and licensed?
+

Our team is held to a high standard of professionalism and preparedness. For specific questions about licensing, training, and certifications relevant to your province or service need, please contact us directly and we will provide the details relevant to your request.

How do I apply for a security position with Amani Protection?
+

We are always looking for dependable, professional individuals to join our team. Visit our Careers page to learn about requirements and submit an application.
