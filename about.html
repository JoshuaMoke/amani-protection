The Story Behind Amani Protection

Joshua Amani Moke

Founder and Director

"Provide reliable, professional protection people can actually count on."

Founded by Joshua Amani Moke, Amani Protection was built from over 10 years of real security experience. After years working in the field and seeing what clients actually need, Joshua started the company three years ago with a simple goal.

He saw a gap between what clients expected and what they were getting. Too many security companies sent unprepared staff, communicated poorly, and treated the work as just a job. Joshua built Amani Protection on the opposite foundation: discipline, professionalism, and genuine care for the people being protected.

Today, Amani Protection serves clients across Canada with a focus on presence, discipline, and peace of mind.
